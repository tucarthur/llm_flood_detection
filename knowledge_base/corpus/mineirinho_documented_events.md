# Documented Mineirinho Creek Flood Event: 12 January 2019

Season: 2018-2019 (within this benchmark's labeled period). Source: "Obras contra
enchentes em Sao Carlos dependem do Governo do Estado e DNIT," Sao Carlos Agora,
http://www.saocarlosagora.com.br/noticia/obras-contra-enchentes-em-sao-carlos-dependem-do-governo-do-estado-e-dnit/15892/
-- verify before citing. On Saturday, 12 January 2019, approximately 65mm of rain fell in
45 minutes over Sao Carlos. Mineirinho Creek overflowed ("o corrego do Mineirinho
transbordou") at the Rotatoria do Cristo, where it converges with Monjolinho and Gregorio
creeks. Avenues in the area were completely covered with water; vehicles were submerged,
and at least one bus became trapped with passengers requiring rescue by the Military
Police and Civil Defense (Defesa Civil).

# Documented Mineirinho Creek Flood Event: 26 November 2020

Season: 2020-2021 (within this benchmark's labeled period). Sources: "Saldo da tragedia:
26 veiculos atingidos e 100 lojas prejudicadas pela chuva em Sao Carlos," Sao Carlos em
Rede, https://saocarlosemrede.com.br/saldo-da-tragedia-26-veiculos-atingidos-e-100-lojas-prejudicadas-pela-chuva-em-sao-carlos/
; and CNN Brasil, "Chuva arrasta carros durante alagamento em Sao Carlos (SP)," 26 Nov
2020 -- verify before citing. On the afternoon of Thursday, 26 November 2020, approximately
138mm of rain fell in roughly 1 hour. News coverage reports the Mineirinho Creek overflowed
("o corrego do Mineirinho ja transbordou") near the Cristo roundabout and the downtown
Baixada do Mercado (municipal market lowland) area. Reported impact: about 150 people
rescued, 26 vehicles damaged, 100-130 commercial establishments flooded, and 38 houses
flooded (2 rendered uninhabitable). This is the most severe documented Mineirinho-specific
event found for this benchmark's labeled period.

# Documented Extreme Rainfall Event Near Mineirinho Creek: 12 January 2020

Season: 2019-2020 (within this benchmark's labeled period). Source: "Defesa Civil estadual
confirma que Sao Carlos foi a mais castigada da regiao," Sao Carlos Agora,
https://www.saocarlosagora.com.br/cidade/defesa-civil-estadual-confirma-que-sao-carlos-foi-a-mais-castigada-da/122050/
-- verify before citing. On Thursday, 12 January 2020, approximately 167.8mm of rain fell
in one day -- reported at the time as the highest daily total in 15 years -- and the
municipality declared a state of emergency. Water reportedly reached 1.5m depth at the
Municipal Market and exceeded 2.5m at the Cristo roundabout, where Mineirinho Creek
converges with Monjolinho and Gregorio creeks; 40 families had homes partially flooded.
News coverage does not explicitly confirm Mineirinho Creek itself overflowed (as opposed
to the downtown area generally), though it is very likely given the location and water
depths reported. Independently, INMET automatic weather station A711 (Sao Carlos) recorded
a concentrated burst of roughly 51mm within a single hour that evening as part of this
event -- a smaller total than the 167.8mm/day figure above (likely because that figure
comes from a different, DAEE-operated gauge; short, intense convective rainfall varies
significantly over even short distances), but confirming a genuine, sharply-peaked, short-
duration rainfall spike consistent with this benchmark's general flash-flood pattern at
this site.
